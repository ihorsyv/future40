Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This is an independent research catalogue of STATION F Future 40 companies. It has no forms, no accounts and no sign-up.

What data we collect

The site has no forms, no accounts and no server-side storage of visitor data.

Analytics

For aggregated visit statistics we use an analytics tool (Umami) that we host on our own infrastructure, not a third-party service. It sets no cookies and collects no identifiable data: the statistics (page views, referrers, device type) stay anonymous and are never passed on to any third party.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps technical request logs that may include IP addresses. Netlify sets their retention period.
