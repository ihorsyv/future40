How to read this catalogue

Every company's inclusion is tied to its named annual STATION F roster, linked on every card. The product description is STATION F's own text from the year of selection — it describes the company then, not necessarily now. Fields such as founders, funding, investors or geography are shown only when a specific primary source confirms that exact field for that exact company; when no such source exists, the field is simply left off the card rather than shown as empty or "unverified". The status pill (Active / Acquired / Closed) is the one exception shown even when unconfirmed — it reads "Status not verified" rather than being omitted, so the absence of a claim is always visible. A confirmed status reflects the date it was checked, not a permanent fact — see each profile for its source and verification date. The sector label is this catalogue's own editorial grouping, not an official STATION F classification.
